🐸 Toad Adventure

A game by Sasha

You start as a teeny tadpole in a pond. Eat snacks, build a fort, and grow!
One day you'll burrow into the ground and become a mini toad...
Then you'll discover the pond was only 1% of a COLOSSAL world.
Collect 🍄 mushrooms and 🌸 petals, make friends, hatch a baby toad, raise a 🌈 rainbow bird, and become the most LEGENDARY TOAD ever!

🍄 Mushroom Shop

You have 0 🍄 and 0 🌸. Golden mushrooms ✨ give free clothes!

🧱 Build your fort

Trade 🪨 stones for fort pieces and furniture. You have 0 stones. Pieces appear in front of you. Press X near a piece to pick it back up.

🏆 The Legendary Race

Who will become the most legendary toad?

🎮 Play Your Games

Play against the toad friends from the world. Win mushrooms 🍄, petals 🌸, and XP!
👑
Boss Race

A one-on-one race against the toad at the top of the Legendary Race board. 3 laps. They are FAST. Beat them for a huge prize and 2 ⭐ stars!

🔨
Hamash

Pick one of 4 arenas: Classic, Watery, Survive Life, or Starry Zoom. Hop in with 9 other toads. Press E to swing! Bonk toads off the edge or knock out all their hearts. The more evolved your toad, the harder you hit. Last toad standing wins!

🏁
Runny Race

Race 7 toads around the track for 6 laps. Grab ⚡ boosters and ❓ power-up boxes, then press E to use them: 🐙 Octopus ink slows a racer, 💣 Bomb must be passed on or you're stunned, 🌈 Rainbow Rain doubles boosters and prizes, 🛡️ Force Field blocks one attack, ✨ Magic Spell freezes a racer!

👅
Fly Catch

60 seconds to eat more flies than 4 toad friends. Run into flies, or press E to shoot your tongue at far ones!

🪷
Lily Pad Hop

Hop across wobbly, moving lily pads all the way across the pond to the golden pad. Fall in and you start over!

☁️
Sky Ring Race

Ride your grown-up magic bird through 12 rings in the sky before the other toads on their birds. Needs a bird that can fly.

🐌
Snail Race

Your pet snail races 3 other snails. Very. Slowly. Mash E to cheer and make it crawl faster! (The first time, you adopt and name your snail.)

🙈
Hide and Seek

6 toad friends hide around the world. Follow the hot-and-cold arrow and find them all in 2 minutes!

Results

🐦 Magic Birds

Find bird eggs hidden around the world. Feed them 🌸 petals to raise them. Only one bird can be equipped at a time!

🆕 Update 6 · September 16, 2026

  • 🐾 Collect Pet! Earn ⭐ stars by winning games, discovering places, and helping toads. Each star hatches a random pet (rarity 1 to 10 stars) for your pet shelf. Take one along with you.
  • 🥷 Pet stealing: every toad friend keeps a pet on a nest. Mark one, sneak over, and hold E for 4 seconds. The owner comes running with their hammer! Toads always carry hammers now, and sometimes bonk you just for fun.
  • 🔨 4 Hamash arenas: Classic, Watery (ponds that slow you and sink you), Survive Life (boulders and lightning), and Starry Zoom (catch falling stars for XP). The arena is much bigger, with 9 opponents and 8 new toad friends.
  • ⚡ Toad friends now steer into the cyan zoom pads in the Runny Race.
  • 👑 Boss Race: a 3-lap duel against the top toad on the Legendary Race board.
  • 🏠 Toad House: build one in your fort, walk inside, and pick your wallpaper.
  • 🌧️ Rain with puddles that give petals, and a rainbow after.
  • 🚕 Dragonfly taxi: fly to any landmark for 1 coin.
  • 🎣 Fishing: buy a rod in the shop and fish from any shore. Catch a Golden Koi for a star!
  • 📱 Fixed the iPad layout: Play Your Games moved to the left so it no longer covers the buttons.

Update 5 · September 13, 2026

  • 🍂 Seasons! Spring, summer, autumn, and winter change every day. Leaves fall in autumn. In winter it snows and the ponds freeze: walk on the ice, slide around, and press Space to twirl (⛸️ coins for fancy twirls).
  • 🐌 Pet snail: adopt and name a snail in the Snail Race. It follows you, very slowly. Mash E to cheer it on in the race.

  • 🎵 Toad Music: buy a drum, flute, bells, or guitar with coins and play with keys 1 to 8. Nearby toads dance!
  • 🏆 6 new trophies.
