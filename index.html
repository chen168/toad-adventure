🐸 Toad Adventure

A game by Sasha

You start as a teeny tadpole in a pond. Eat snacks, build a fort, and grow!
One day you'll burrow into the ground and become a mini toad...
Then you'll discover the pond was only 1% of a COLOSSAL world.
Collect 🍄 mushrooms and 🌸 petals, make friends, hatch a baby toad, raise a 🌈 rainbow bird, and become the most LEGENDARY TOAD ever!

Hamash

Grab your hammer and hop into the arena with 6 other toads. Press E to swing! Bonk toads off the edge or knock out all their hearts. The more evolved your toad, the harder you hit. Last toad standing wins!

Runny Race

Race 7 toads around the track for 6 laps. Grab ⚡ boosters and ❓ power-up boxes, then press E to use them: 🐙 Octopus ink slows a racer, 💣 Bomb must be passed on or you're stunned, 🌈 Rainbow Rain doubles boosters and prizes, 🛡️ Force Field blocks one attack, ✨ Magic Spell freezes a racer!
